Bridging the Gap Between Concept and Completion

LC Fabricating, Inc. is a Warsaw, Indiana-based company led by Jim McDairmant, offering decades of hands-on experience in custom fabrication, product design, and outdoor living solutions.

With a background spanning glass production engineering, custom canvas and vinyl work, retractable awning systems, and CAD-based technical design, Jim brings a rare combination of practical shop-floor knowledge and professional engineering capability to every project.

Whether you need a custom outdoor enclosure, precision shop drawings, or full project management from concept to delivery — LC Fabricating has the expertise to make it happen.
