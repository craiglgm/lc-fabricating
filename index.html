Bridging the Gap Between Concept and Completion

LC Fabricating, Inc. is a Warsaw, Indiana-based company led by Jim McDairmant, offering decades of hands-on experience in custom aluminum and steel fabrication, product design, and outdoor living solutions.

With a background spanning glass production, custom canvas work, and retractable systems, Jim combines practical shop-floor knowledge with 3D modeling, rendering, and technical design. Specializing in bespoke stairs and railings, LC Fabricating excels at transforming complex architectural concepts into high-precision, finished installs.

Whether you need a custom outdoor enclosure, precision shop drawings, or full project management from concept to delivery—LC Fabricating has the manufacturing expertise to make it happen.
